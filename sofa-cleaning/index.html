SOFAS, COUCHES AND SETS

Seat by seat,
in your living room.

A sofa set in a Kenyan living room collects tea, juice, dust and the traffic of a household. We clean it in your home. There is no need to send the furniture anywhere.

Say whether you have a two-seater, a corner suite or a 7-seater. The seat count changes the time and the quote. Fabric, leather and mixed suites are priced from what you describe, and photos help.

Upholstery cleaning on this page also covers fabric dining chairs and loose cushions when they are part of the same visit. Tell us the count.

Fabric sofas

The usual request: a fabric couch or a full sofa set with cushions. Mention stains you want treated.

Leather

Leather is a different process from cloth. Say so in the first message so the quote is for the right job.

Sofa sets

L-shaped suites and 7-seaters. Count the seats, including the ones that do not look dirty.

Chairs and cushions

Dining chairs and spare cushions cleaned with the sofa when you want the room handled together.

MATTRESSES

Mattress cleaning
with the same visit.

Mattress cleaning is a common add-on when the sofa is already being done, or on its own when a bed needs attention. Clients have asked us to refresh mattresses along with carpets. Tell us the bed size and how many mattresses.

If the reason is bed bugs rather than stains or dust, start with fumigation and mention the mattress. A clean and a pest treatment solve different problems, and we will quote the one you actually need.

Sofa cleaning prices in Kenya follow the seats, the material and the staining. A mattress adds its own line. We send the figure after we have those details. Nothing on this page is a fixed tariff.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions we hear
before a quote.

Do you offer sofa cleaning in Nairobi?

Yes. Send the neighbourhood, the material and the number of seats. We confirm the visit before it is booked.

Can you clean a whole sofa set?

Yes. A sofa set, including larger 7-seaters, is a normal request. The seat count belongs in the enquiry.

Do you clean leather couches?

Leather can be booked. It is quoted separately from fabric because the work is different.

Do you offer mattress cleaning?

Yes, on its own or with the sofa. Tell us how many mattresses and the sizes.

How much does sofa cleaning cost in Kenya?

The price depends on seats, material and staining. Send those details for a quote. We do not list a single sofa price here.

Which towns can request sofa cleaning?

Nairobi, Thika, Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret and the other towns on our location list. Your exact address is confirmed in the reply.
