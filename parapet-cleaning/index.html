WHAT THE JOB IS

The outside wall,
not the corridor mop.

Parapet cleaning services in Nairobi are usually for mid-rise and high-rise buildings: balcony fronts, rooftop parapets and the exterior walls around them. It is outdoor work. Interior office cleaning and house cleaning are separate bookings.

Access has to be safe for that building. The visit might be from a balcony, from the roof, or by another route the caretaker or property manager allows. We agree the route before we set a date. We do not arrive and invent a way onto a facade.

This page is for booking the clean. Staff vacancies are not advertised here.

Apartment blocks

Balcony parapets and shared exterior walls. A manager may need to approve access even when a resident makes the enquiry.

Office buildings

Street-facing parapets ahead of a tenant moving in, a sale, or a scheduled wash.

Rooftop edges

The low wall around a roof terrace. Tell us about water supply and who holds the roof key.

Single homes

A house balcony that has gone dark in the rain. Say how many sides and whether a ladder is already on site.

WHAT TO SEND

Height, access
and a photo.

Include these details so the quote is for the right building.

  • The town and the estate or road
  • Floors, or the number of balconies to be washed
  • Who can grant roof or balcony access
  • Whether water is available on that level
  • Photos of the parapet from the ground and, if you have them, from the balcony

Parapet cleaning prices depend on the area, the height and the access. We quote after those facts are clear. There is no flat national rate on this page.

If the building also needs an indoor office clean or a pest treatment, mention office cleaning or fumigation in the same message and we will separate the quotes.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions we hear
before a quote.

What is parapet cleaning?

It is the cleaning of parapet walls: the low walls at roof edges and balcony fronts, and often the exterior band around them. It is outside work, planned around safe access.

Do you offer parapet cleaning in Nairobi?

Yes. Send the neighbourhood, the height and how the wall can be reached. We confirm the building before we book.

Do you work outside Nairobi?

Enquiries from other Kenyan towns are welcome. Use the town pages below and write parapet cleaning as the service.

How much do parapet cleaning services cost?

Height, area and access set the price. We quote the building you describe. This page has no fixed fee.

Can a resident book, or only a manager?

Either person can enquire. Shared exterior walls often need the building's permission, and we will ask for that before the visit.

Is this a vacancy listing?

No. This page is for requesting parapet cleaning. It does not advertise jobs.
