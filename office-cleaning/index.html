WHAT AN OFFICE CLEAN COVERS

Desks, washrooms
and the kitchenette.

Office cleaning services in Nairobi are planned around the people in the building. A reception, workstations, meeting rooms, a kitchenette, washrooms and bins are the usual brief. Add or remove rooms when you write.

Carpeted floors can be part of the regular clean or a separate carpet cleaning visit when the pile needs more than a vacuum. The exterior parapet of an office block is parapet cleaning. A pest problem in a kitchen or store is fumigation. Name them if you want those quotes too.

Tell us if we should use products already kept on site or bring cleaning products. We confirm that when we quote, so the first visit is not a guess.

Daily or weekly

A standing visit on the days you choose. Say the start time you prefer.

One-off cleans

Before a new tenant, after a stocktake, or when the office has fallen behind.

Shops and studios

Smaller commercial rooms, counters and washrooms. The same enquiry form covers them.

After hours

Share the evening or early morning window. We confirm whether that slot can be staffed.

A SCHEDULE THAT FITS

Your hours,
then our reply.

Some offices want the clean finished before staff arrive. Others want it after closing. Write the window you have in mind. We only agree a time we can actually keep.

Office cleaning prices follow the floor area, the number of washrooms and how many visits you want each week. A shop with one room is a different quote from a full floor. Send the size and the frequency. There is no printed tariff here.

A home office inside a house is usually house cleaning. Use this page when the space is a workplace with its own entrance, staff or customers.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions we hear
before a quote.

Do you offer office cleaning services in Nairobi?

Yes. Send the neighbourhood, the size and how often you want a visit. We confirm the address and the timing.

Can you clean before we open or after we close?

Share the hours you want. We accept the slot only when we can staff it.

Do you clean shops and small offices?

Yes. Counters, floors and washrooms in a small commercial space are a normal brief.

Do you bring cleaning products?

Tell us your preference. We confirm in the quote whether products come with us or are already on site.

How much do office cleaning services cost?

Floor area, washrooms and visits per week set the price. Send those details for a quote.

Can the same team clean the parapet outside?

Exterior parapet cleaning is quoted separately. Mention it if the building needs both.
