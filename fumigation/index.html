WHAT FUMIGATION MEANS

A pest treatment,
arranged for your building.

In Kenya, people usually say fumigation when they want a property treated for insects. The visit may use a spray, a mist or a fog. The method depends on the pest and on where it is living, which is why we ask before we quote.

A fumigation visit is a different booking from a house clean or an office clean. Pair them when you want both, and we will say which part of the quote covers which job.

One visit is a treatment, not a lifetime guarantee. If food is left open, drains stay wet or gaps let insects back in, they can return. We will say that plainly when it applies to your place.

PESTS PEOPLE ASK ABOUT

Cockroaches, bed bugs
and the rest.

Name what you are seeing. A kitchen full of cockroaches, bed bugs in a bedroom, ants along a skirting board, fleas after pets, or mosquitoes in a room that will not settle: each one changes how we plan the visit.

Bed bugs often involve the mattress and the sofa as well as the room. If you want those cleaned rather than treated, see sofa and upholstery cleaning and tell us in the same message.

We carry out the treatment and bring the equipment for that visit. This page does not sell fumigation machines or list machine prices. If you were comparing equipment to buy, the service we offer is the visit itself.

Cockroach fumigation

Kitchens, drains and shop stores. Tell us how widespread it is and whether the space is a home or a food business.

Bed bug fumigation

Bedrooms, guest rooms and lodgings. Say how many rooms are affected and whether furniture can be moved.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions we hear

What is fumigation?

It is a pest-control treatment for a room or a whole property, using a spray, mist or fog chosen for the insect and the building. It is booked separately from an ordinary house clean.

Do you offer fumigation services in Nairobi?

Yes. Send the neighbourhood, the pest and the type of property. We confirm that we can reach the address before anything is booked.

Do you treat cockroaches and bed bugs?

Those are two of the most common reasons people enquire. Name the pest in your message so the quote matches the job.

How much do fumigation services cost in Kenya?

We quote after we know the rooms, the pest and whether a follow-up is needed. There is no single price on this page.

Do you sell fumigation machines?

No. We provide the fumigation visit. We do not sell machines or publish machine prices.

Can you fumigate an office or a shop?

Send the workplace details and the hours you can give us access. We will say whether that timing works.
