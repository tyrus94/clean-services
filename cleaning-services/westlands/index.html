Yes. Share the building name, the floor and whether there is a lift. Many Westlands bookings are one- and two-bedroom flats. Some are offices on Waiyaki Way or Mpaka Road, which you should label as an office.

Can you fumigate one Westlands flat rather than the whole block?

We fumigate the unit you book. Tell us whether you are seeing cockroaches, bed bugs or something else, and whether anyone in the flat needs us to know about the treatment. We will say what access we need before agreeing a time.

Do you offer carpet cleaning in Westlands?

Yes. Fitted carpets and loose rugs in homes and small offices can be booked alone or with a house clean. The carpet cleaning page explains the service. Then tell us which rooms.
