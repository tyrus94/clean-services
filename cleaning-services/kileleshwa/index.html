IN THIS PART OF NAIROBI

Which Kileleshwa
road you are on.

Gatundu Road, Othaya Road and Mandera Road are the maisonette streets. Riverside Drive is the edge toward the river and toward Westlands. Those are different drives, so name the road.

Maisonette move-outs

Courts in Kileleshwa change tenants without looking any different from the road. Tell us the day the house must be ready and whether it will be empty.

Shared-garden kitchens

Fumigation requests often start in a kitchen that opens onto a shared garden. Say what you have seen, and whether you want one room or the whole house.

Stairs and landings

Carpet cleaning in these houses usually includes a staircase, not just a living-room rug. Mention the stairs so the visit is planned for them.

Kileleshwa does not have its own shopping-mall landmark the way Kilimani has Yaya Centre. The road name is the useful detail.

Nearby areas

Lavington and Kilimani share Kileleshwa's borders. Westlands is beyond Riverside, toward Waiyaki Way, if that is where the flat actually is.

GOOD TO KNOW

Kileleshwa cleaning:
common questions

Do you clean maisonettes on Gatundu Road and Othaya Road?

Yes. Those roads, with Mandera Road, are the older Kileleshwa houses. Tell us the road, the number of bedrooms and whether the house is empty for a move-out clean.

What about flats toward Riverside?

Riverside Drive is the Kileleshwa edge toward the river. If the flat is on that side, say Riverside in the message so we do not assume a maisonette on Gatundu Road.

Do you fumigate in Kileleshwa?

Yes, for the house or flat you book. Tell us the pest and whether the kitchen opens onto a shared garden, which is common in the maisonette courts.

Which nearby areas have their own pages?

Lavington, Kilimani and Westlands each have a page, because they sit against Kileleshwa. Use the page that matches the property, not the nearest shopping centre.
