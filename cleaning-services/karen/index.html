IN THIS PART OF NAIROBI

Find the house,
then clean it.

The Hub Karen is in Karen. Galleria is on Langata Road and is the landmark people use when they are describing the Langata Road side. Either helps. Neither replaces the road name.

Cottages and main houses

Say whether the clean includes only the main house or also a cottage. Leaving that out is the usual reason a Karen visit is mis-sized.

Carpets in large rooms

Karen living rooms can take a full carpet clean on their own. Tell us the number of carpeted rooms rather than a vague whole house.

Pests at the edge of the plot

If you want fumigation, say whether the problem is inside the house or also outside. We agree the scope before anyone comes.

Karen is not next to the CBD, and it is not next to Lavington either. Dagoretti sits between Lavington and Karen along Ngong Road.

Nearby areas

Lavington is the closer western estate back toward the city, with Dagoretti in between. South C is a different direction, along Langata Road past Langata and Wilson Airport. Kilimani is the apartment district further in along Ngong Road.

GOOD TO KNOW

Karen cleaning:
common questions

Do you clean large Karen houses, or only apartments?

Karen bookings are usually houses on Karen Road, Hardy, Bogani Road, Miotoni and the lanes off them. Tell us the number of bedrooms and whether a cottage is included. Apartments are fine too if you say that is what you have.

Can you fumigate a Karen house?

We treat the house you book. If you are also seeing pests outside, describe where, so we know what you are asking for. We confirm the scope before the visit.

Is Galleria or The Hub Karen the right landmark?

Both help. Galleria is on Langata Road and The Hub is in Karen. Still send the road or court. The team comes to the house, not to the mall.

Is South C the same area as Karen?

No. South C is further along Langata Road, toward Nyayo Stadium, and it has its own page. Use this page for Karen. If the house is in Langata and you are not sure of the estate name, name the road in the message.
