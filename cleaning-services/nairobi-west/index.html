between the city and South C.

Madaraka is toward the city. Wilson Airport is the landmark before Karen. South C is the neighbouring estate, past Nyayo Stadium. Use those to place the house, then give the close.

Madaraka or Nairobi West

They sit next to each other on Langata Road. Say which. A Madaraka flat and a Nairobi West maisonette should not share a one-line address.

Wilson Airport side

Houses toward the airport are still Nairobi West, not Karen. Karen is further along Langata Road and has its own page.

Parapet on maisonettes

Many maisonettes here have a parapet along the roof edge. Mention it only if parapet cleaning is the job you want. It is separate from an indoor clean.

South C Shopping Centre and Mugoya belong on the South C page. Nairobi West Shopping Centre belongs on this one.

Do you clean houses in Nairobi West and Madaraka?

Yes. Say which estate, the close or road, and the bedroom count. Langata Road and Wilson Airport are fine as landmarks, and the house still needs its own directions.

Is Nairobi West the same as South C?

No. South C, including Mugoya, has its own page. Nairobi West is the estate toward the city, on the Langata Road side of South C. Use the page that matches the house.

Do you offer fumigation, carpet, sofa and parapet cleaning here?

Yes. Fumigation and pest control, carpet cleaning, sofa cleaning and parapet cleaning can be requested as well as a house or office clean. Name the service. A shop near the shopping centre should be labelled as a workplace.
