Yes. Say which estate, the court or road, and the bedroom count. A Pipeline flat and a Fedha maisonette need different access, so the estate name matters.

Can you clean an office or store near JKIA or the Industrial Area?

Yes, if you book it as a workplace. Tell us whether it is an office, a shop or a store, and whether staff will be there. A house clean and a workplace clean are not the same visit.

Yes. Fumigation, carpet cleaning and sofa cleaning can be booked on their own or with a house clean. Name the pest, or the rooms, so we know which service you want. Parapet cleaning is separate, for an exterior parapet wall.
