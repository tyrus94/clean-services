IN THIS PART OF NAIROBI

The gate is part
of the booking.

Ruaka is the town across Limuru Road, in Kiambu, and it has its own page. If the property is in Ruaka rather than inside Runda, use the Ruaka page. Two Rivers is the mall on that junction, not an estate phase.

Gate access

Tell us the phase, the close and who will clear the team in. A Runda booking that omits the gate is not a complete booking.

Before guests

Fumigation and a house clean are often requested together before visitors. They are still two services. Name both if you want both.

Carpets in reception rooms

Estate houses often want the formal living room carpet cleaned on its own. Say which rooms, rather than assuming the whole house is carpeted.

Runda sits between Limuru Road and Kiambu Road. It is not the same place as Gigiri or Village Market, which are on the Kiambu Road page.

Nearby areas
