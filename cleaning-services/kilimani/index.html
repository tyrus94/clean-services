GOOD TO KNOW

Kilimani cleaning:
common questions

Do you do carpet cleaning in Kilimani?

Yes. Tell us how many rooms and whether the carpet is fitted or loose. It can be booked on its own. See the carpet cleaning page for what the service covers, then send the flat details.

Is fumigation in Kilimani something you can arrange?

Yes, for the flat or office you are responsible for. Say which pest you are seeing, especially if it is cockroaches or bed bugs, and which floor the unit is on.

Can you clean a Kilimani apartment before I hand back the keys?

That is a move-out clean. Tell us the number of bedrooms, whether it is furnished, and the day the keys have to go back. Yaya Centre, Argwings Kodhek Road and Adams Arcade are all useful landmarks.

Do you also cover Hurlingham and Woodley?

They sit against Kilimani and do not have their own pages. If the building is there rather than on Argwings Kodhek Road, say so and we will confirm the visit from that location. Kileleshwa and Lavington do have their own pages.
