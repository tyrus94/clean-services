Use South C Shopping Centre or Nyayo Stadium as the landmark, then the close. Nairobi West is the next estate toward the city and has its own page. Use that page if the house is in Nairobi West or Madaraka.

Mugoya houses

If you are in Mugoya, say Mugoya rather than only South C. It is the part of the estate most directions start from, and it is specific enough to route a visit.

Parking in the court

GOOD TO KNOW

South C cleaning:
common questions

Do you clean houses in Mugoya and the rest of South C?

Yes. Mugoya is the part of South C most people name first. If you are elsewhere in South C, say the road or the close, and use South C Shopping Centre or Nyayo Stadium as the landmark.

Is Nairobi West covered on this page?

Nairobi West is the next estate toward the city and has its own page, covering Madaraka and the Wilson Airport side of Langata Road. Use that page if the house is there. This page is for South C, including Mugoya.

Do you offer the same services as in South B?

House cleaning, office cleaning where there is a workplace, fumigation, carpet, sofa and parapet cleaning can all be requested. The location still needs to be South C, or a place we have agreed. South B has its own page.

How far is Karen from a South C booking?

Far enough to be a different page. Karen is along Langata Road past Wilson Airport. If the house is in Karen, use the Karen page. If it is in South C, stay on this one.
