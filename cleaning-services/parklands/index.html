GOOD TO KNOW

Parklands cleaning:
common questions

Do you clean apartments in Highridge and the rest of Parklands?

Yes. Share the building, the floor and whether there is a lift. Highridge includes houses as well as flats. Say which you have.

Is Parklands the same booking as Westlands?

No. Westlands is the next centre, around Sarit Centre, Rhapta Road and Waiyaki Way, and it has its own page. Parklands is the flats and offices toward City Park and the museum. Use the page that matches the building.

Do you clean offices in Parklands, and carpets in the flats?

Yes. Office cleaning is for a workplace, and you should say when staff will be out. Carpet cleaning and sofa cleaning can be booked for a flat on their own or with a house clean. Parapet cleaning is only for an exterior parapet wall.

How do I request cleaning services in Parklands?
