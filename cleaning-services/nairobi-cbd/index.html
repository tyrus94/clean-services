Nairobi CBD cleaning:
common questions

Are you one of the cleaning companies in Nairobi CBD?

We clean properties in the CBD. We do not publish a shop address there. The work happens at your office, shop or building. Call or WhatsApp +254 706 855633 and say which street you are on.

Can a CBD office be cleaned when staff have left?

Ask for the time you need. Many CBD enquiries are for the evening, or for a shop after a stocktake. We confirm the slot when we know the building. This page does not list fixed hours.

Do you clean parapet walls on CBD buildings?

Parapet cleaning is one of our services, and streets such as Kenyatta Avenue and Moi Avenue are where that request comes from. Tell us the building and which frontage you mean. The details are agreed before anyone goes up.

What if the office is in Upper Hill, not the CBD?

Say Upper Hill. It is next to the CBD, but access is different and it is not the area this page describes. We would rather have the right location than a landmark that is only nearby.
