IN THIS PART OF NAIROBI

Name the end
of the road.

Village Market is the mall at Gigiri. Muthaiga Golf Club's neighbourhood is the city end. The Northern Bypass is the far end of the stretch this page is for. Past that, you may be in Kiambu County rather than Nairobi.

Muthaiga houses

These are older residential plots at the start of Kiambu Road. A house clean here is closer, in distance, to Westlands than to Village Market. Say Muthaiga if that is the right end.

Gigiri and Village Market

GOOD TO KNOW

Kiambu Road cleaning:
common questions

Do you cover the whole of Kiambu Road?

We take enquiries from Muthaiga at the city end, through Gigiri and Village Market, and out toward the Northern Bypass. Name the landmark nearest the property. Muthaiga and Village Market are not the same trip.

Is Runda included on this page?

Runda has its own page, because it is an estate off the road rather than an address on Kiambu Road. If the house is inside Runda, use that page. If it is on Kiambu Road or in Gigiri, use this one.

Do you clean offices around Gigiri as well as houses?

Yes. Office cleaning and house cleaning are both available. Say which it is, and whether the office needs to be empty when we arrive.

What if the property is in Kiambu town, not on this road?

Use the Kiambu town page, or name Kiambu town in the message. This page is for the Nairobi stretch through Muthaiga and Gigiri, not for every address in Kiambu County.
