Ridgeways and Garden Estate

GOOD TO KNOW

Kiambu Road cleaning:
common questions

Do you cover the whole of Kiambu Road?

We take enquiries along the Nairobi stretch of Kiambu Road from Karura and Ridgeways, through Ciata City and Fourways Junction, out toward the Northern Bypass. Name the estate or mall nearest the property.

Is Runda, Gigiri or Village Market included on this page?

No. Runda sits between Kiambu Road and Limuru Road and has its own page. Gigiri and Village Market are on Limuru Road, not Kiambu Road, and Muthaiga is its own estate at the city end. Mention the exact area when you message us.

Do you clean offices along Kiambu Road as well as houses?

Yes. Office cleaning and house cleaning are both available for premises near Ridgeways Mall, Ciata City and along the corridor. Say which it is, and whether the office needs to be empty when we arrive.

What if the property is in Kiambu town, not on this road?

Use the Kiambu town page, or name Kiambu town in the message. This page is for the Nairobi stretch of Kiambu Road up to the Northern Bypass, not for every address in Kiambu County.
