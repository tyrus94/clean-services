IN THIS PART OF NAIROBI

South B is not
South C.

They are neighbours, and the shopping centres have similar names. South B Shopping Centre and Bellevue belong on this page. South C Shopping Centre and Mugoya belong on the South C page. Sending the wrong one sends the team to the wrong estate.

Court handovers

Move-out cleans in South B need the day the house must be ready and whether it will be empty. A furnished clean and an empty handover are different.

Ground-floor kitchens

Fumigation requests here often start on the ground floor. Say what you have seen and whether it is one room or the whole maisonette.

Roof-edge parapets

Many South B maisonettes have a parapet along the flat roof. If that is the job, say which side of the building. It is a separate service from an indoor clean.

The Industrial Area is the landmark on the CBD side of South B. It is not where the houses are. Give a court inside the estate.

Nearby areas

South C is the next estate. The CBD is to the north, beyond the Industrial Area and the railway. Kilimani is across the city, not a neighbouring estate, so it is not listed here.
